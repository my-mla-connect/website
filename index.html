    Crosdesk

    AI-enabledMCP serverSelf-hosted or cloud

    One project. Many departments.

    Project management for organisations where several departments deliver the same project. Draw the work on a canvas, press Verify, and Crosdesk computes the dates. Each department plans its part into its own sprints, sees only what it should, and the dashboard shows where the plan slipped and why.

    • A node-based canvas that turns into a dated plan, with a baseline to measure slips against
    • Department sprints, backlogs, epics, features with approvals, and releases
    • Thirteen reports, each answering one question honestly, plus a department scorecard
    • Meetings, todos, versioned documents and bug recordings with console and network logs, all on the work itself
    Built for AI agents Crosdesk ships an MCP server. Connect Claude, Cursor or any MCP client and it works as you, in one workspace, with exactly your access, and every change it makes is in the audit log.

    FilesTrace

    AI summariesRTI registerSelf-hosted

    Where is this file, what's its status, and who did what to it?

    File tracking for government departments and organisations that run on paperwork. A citizen sends a message or a photo of a form; it comes back as a summarised file with the department, status and next desk already suggested.

    • Messages and documents summarised by AI, with department and next desk suggested
    • Departments, wards and a status lifecycle you configure yourself, no code needed
    • An append-only history of every comment, forward and status change
    • Group-based access, a shared calendar and an RTI Act register
    • Self-hosted on your own PostgreSQL, mobile-first, with matching PDF exports

    Questions

    The things people ask first.

    Answered here so the first conversation can start somewhere more interesting.

    What does Buddhyantra actually do?

    We build web applications and mobile apps, and that is where most engagements start. Around them we build citizen connect platforms, add AI that handles routing, triage, follow-up and reporting, and train your team to use AI well. We also make two products of our own, Crosdesk and FilesTrace.

    What is Crosdesk?

    Crosdesk is our AI-enabled project management product for organisations where several departments deliver the same project. It has an MCP server, so Claude, Cursor or any MCP client can read and update work as you, with exactly your access. You can find it at crosdesk.com.

    What does your AI training cover?

    It depends on who is in the room. For all staff: writing good prompts and checking what comes back. For engineers: AI-assisted development, agents, tools and MCP. For leaders: where AI is worth the risk, and how to handle public and personal data. Sessions are hands-on and use your own work as the material.

    Do you build mobile apps for both Android and iOS?

    Yes. We build for both stores, plus progressive web apps where an install is more than people need. Where the network is unreliable, we design the app to keep working offline and sync later.

    Do you work with government departments?

    Yes, and it is a large part of what we do. Public offices carry the hardest version of the problem: statutory deadlines and an audit trail that has to hold up. FilesTrace, our file tracking and RTI register product, came directly out of that work.

    How does an AI consulting engagement start?

    With the problem, not the model. We look at where work piles up, test a small prototype on your real data, and only then recommend a build. If a rule, a form or a better process would do the job, we'll say so.
